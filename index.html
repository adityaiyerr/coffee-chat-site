Transparency

How your Gmail connection works

To deliver your personalized outreach emails, we connect to your Gmail account using an App Password that you generate yourself through Google's official security settings. Your main account password is never shared with us. The App Password connects through Gmail's standard IMAP and SMTP protocols, the same way Apple Mail or Outlook would connect to your inbox.

✓

What We have access to

  • ■ Composing and saving emails as drafts in your account
  • ■ Sending emails on your behalf only when you explicitly approve
  • ■ Reading delivery confirmations to track which emails sent successfully
−

What We never touch

  • ■ Reading your existing emails or message content
  • ■ Your main account password or recovery codes
  • ■ Contacts, calendar, or other Google services
  • ■ Storing or saving any of your email data

By default, all outreach emails are loaded as drafts in your Gmail account so you can review and send each one yourself. You can revoke our access at any time by deleting the App Password from your Google account security settings — no support request needed.
