FAQ

Common questions

Where does the data come from?

Licensed data providers combined with independent verification. No single source is complete — sourcing across multiple databases means fewer gaps in coverage and fewer contacts you'd otherwise miss.

What does "verified" mean?

Each email address is tested against the recipient mail server before delivery. Verified contacts had their address confirmed as real and actively accepting mail. They go in the Verified tab.

What's a catch-all domain?

Some mail servers are configured to accept any incoming email, even to addresses that don't exist. Because the server never bounces, we can't confirm whether the specific inbox is real. These contacts are flagged Risky so you can decide whether to include them in your sends.

Do you send emails on my behalf?

No. The list is yours to use however you like in whatever sequencer or CRM you run. I source and verify the contacts; you own and run the outreach.

How long does delivery take?

[X business days] from ICP sign-off, depending on list size and ICP complexity. Turnaround time is confirmed on the intake call before any work begins.

I already have contacts in my CRM. Will I get duplicates?

Share your existing list and I'll deduplicate before delivery. You receive only net-new contacts that aren't already in your system.
