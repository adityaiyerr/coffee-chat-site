FAQ

Common questions

Where does the data come from?

Licensed data providers combined with independent verification. No single source is complete — sourcing across multiple databases means fewer gaps and fewer contacts you'd otherwise miss.

What does "verified" mean?

Each email is tested against the recipient mail server before delivery. Verified contacts had their address confirmed as real and actively accepting mail. They go in the Verified tab.

What's a catch-all domain?

Some mail servers accept email to any address at that domain, even ones that don't exist. Because the server never bounces, we can't confirm the specific inbox. These are flagged Risky so you can decide how to handle them.

Do you send emails on my behalf?

No. The list is yours to use in whatever tool you run. I source and verify the contacts — you own and run the outreach.

How long does delivery take?

[X business days] from ICP sign-off, depending on list size and complexity. Turnaround time is confirmed on the call before any work begins.

I already have contacts in my CRM. Will I get duplicates?

Share your existing list and I'll deduplicate before delivery. You receive only net-new contacts not already in your system.
